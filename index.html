Corporate Overview

Company Profile

Magnesco Electrical Stamping Pvt. Ltd., a NABCB accredited ISO 9001:2015 company well known in Motor manufacturing Industries since 1995. We started our journey in 1995 as a proprietorship company named ‘Magnesco’ and now we are a Private Limited company named ‘Magnesco Electrical Stamping Pvt. Ltd.’ registered with ROC on 31.03.2008.

We are also an MSME registered Organization. We are a growing company currently producing 800 MT and aimed to reach 1,600 MT in the next operational cycle. We are an approved vendor for the manufacturing of Stator & Rotor Stamping for Traction Motor type 6FRA 6068 & 6FXA 7059 by CLW.

We operate two production houses at Howrah, West Bengal with a dedicated engineering work force of 50 plus, spreading over industrial working spaces of around 17,000 sq. ft., equipped with complete in-house R&D and precision Tool Room ecosystems.
